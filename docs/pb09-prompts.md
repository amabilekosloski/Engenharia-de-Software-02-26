## Como gerar

1. Abra uma conversa nova no ChatGPT.
2. Anexe `pagina01` e `pagina02` e envie o **bloco de referência** abaixo.
3. Em seguida, cole **um prompt de cada vez**, na ordem dos quadros.
4. Peça 2 versões de cada quadro e escolha a melhor.
5. Depois do quadro 8, **anexe a imagem escolhida** (que mostra a moradora) nos quadros em que ela aparece (9 a 12, 20 e 22).

### Bloco de referência (enviar primeiro, junto com as imagens anexadas)

```
Use the attached images as reference for the characters, outfits, art
style and the garden house. Keep Nina, Ravi, their maintenance
jumpsuits (moss-green for Nina, blue for Ravi), the house, the porch
and the garden exactly consistent with the references.

Characters:
Nina: young adult Brazilian woman, brown skin, shoulder-length curly
dark brown hair, dark eyes, moss-green maintenance jumpsuit.
Ravi: tall young adult man, light skin, short straight black hair,
brown eyes, blue maintenance jumpsuit with grey details, tool belt.
Local woman: mature woman, about 50, tan skin, dark hair with grey
streaks tied back, worn olive utility vest over a patched shirt,
weathered kind face. Keep her appearance the same in every panel.

I will send one panel prompt at a time. Reply with one image per prompt.
```
